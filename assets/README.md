These images belong to the VideoLoad product showcase. This repository does not grant an open-source license to the VideoLoad application, its branding or its product assets.

- `app-icon.png`: official VideoLoad app icon, resized for the product README.
- `screenshots/player.png`: real iOS app screenshot showing clip marks.
- `screenshots/library.png`: real iOS app screenshot showing the local library.

Screenshots were captured for the iOS 1.0.6 product presentation on November 10, 2026, and resized without changing their content. iOS is coming soon; Android UI differs. The video pictured inside the app is a developer-created geometric animation with original synthesized audio, not third-party film or stock media. Its [public demo page](https://fcjnotbad-source.github.io/videoload-review-media/ios/review.html) explains the permitted use of those demo files.

To introduce the product, link to the official repository and download entries so readers can find current information. For other asset-use requests, use the [developer contact page](https://vdb.aivest.art/apps/jiange-kuaixia#contact).
