# VideoLoad product film

[Watch on the official website](https://videoload.pdos.work/#film) · [Download MP4](../assets/promo/videoload-overview-en.mp4) · [English transcript](../assets/promo/transcript.en.txt) · [English captions](../assets/promo/videoload-overview-en.vtt)

The 54-second film explains the mobile video download browser through downloading, watching, trimming, free everyday use, no app login or in-app ads, locally stored browsing/activity records without collection or uploads to our servers, and independently controlled dual playback. It promotes the free official website edition. Store editions can have different feature access.

The video is 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC stereo audio and English narration. English subtitles are burned into the video; VTT and SRT files are also provided. The final audio measures approximately −15.9 LUFS. Final media is optimized for progressive playback.

## Media and voice

App interfaces are real VideoLoad captures. The geometric sample video was created by the developer. The two women in the dual-player demonstration are fictional adult AI portraits, labeled as demo imagery in the film. The portraits were created with the built-in image-generation tool, using this brief: two independent horizontal lifestyle portraits, one adult East Asian woman in an ivory blouse in a bright studio and one adult brunette in a blue casual jacket in a sunlit garden, both naturally lit, fully clothed, with no text or logos.

The English voice follows the requested product-operations workflow: a consistent grounded male reference voice, natural pacing, supported sentence endings, gentle processing and loudness normalization. The background score and transition accents are original synthesized sounds. Reference voice recordings and production caches are not part of the public media.

## Validation

The animation passed runtime, motion, contrast and layout checks; intentional scene-transition overlap was reviewed through snapshots. Eleven keyframes and five frames from the final encode were visually inspected. Container, dimensions, frame rate, audio codec, subtitle timing and final loudness were checked. Public assets are listed in [metadata.json](../assets/promo/metadata.json).

The privacy segment now focuses on browsing history and activity records, rather than media uploads. The replacement narration and subtitles were checked against the generated audio; the rest of the audio remains unchanged. The website dual-player image also uses the two labeled fictional adult AI portraits.
