# Quick start / 快速上手

[Home](../README.md) · [中文首页](../README.zh-CN.md) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

### Save and play your first video

1. [Install from an official entry](downloads.md).
2. In VideoLoad's browser, open the [developer-created demo page](https://fcjnotbad-source.github.io/videoload-review-media/ios/review.html). Its MP4 is authorized for downloading and testing in the app.
3. Use the floating download button and choose the MP4 resource. Open the download center and wait for it to complete.
4. Open the video to play it. Try seeking and changing speed.

### Keep just the moments you need

1. Open the clip/scissors controls in the player.
2. Mark a start, then an end. For the 16-second demo, try 1–3 seconds and 9–11 seconds.
3. Choose marked-only playback to revisit those moments. You can repeat your selected moments; loop controls differ by platform. Marking keeps the original video intact.

### Go further with Pro

Import your own videos from the system picker, choose two videos for comparison, or export selected moments from a compatible source as MP4 or GIF. On iOS, GIF selections can total up to 30 seconds; local HLS offline packages cannot be exported. Menus differ between Android and iOS.

If something does not work, check the [FAQ](faq.md) or [send feedback](https://github.com/fcjnotbad-source/videoload-app/issues/new/choose) with your platform, app version and steps.

## 简体中文

### 保存并播放第一个视频

1. [通过官方入口安装](downloads.md#简体中文)。
2. 在 VideoLoad 内置浏览器打开[开发者原创演示页](https://fcjnotbad-source.github.io/videoload-review-media/ios/review-zh.html)，其中的 MP4 已获授权用于下载和应用测试。
3. 点击悬浮下载按钮，选择 MP4 资源，在下载中心等待完成。
4. 打开视频，尝试拖动进度条和调整倍速。

### 留下想反复看的片段

1. 打开播放器中的片段／剪刀工具。
2. 标记起点和终点。可以在 16 秒演示视频中试着标记 1–3 秒、9–11 秒两段。
3. 选择只播标注，直接回看重点；可循环观看所选片段，具体控制方式因平台而异。标注不会修改原视频。

### 用 Pro 继续探索

通过系统选择器导入自己的视频，选择两个视频双屏对照，或把兼容来源的已标注片段导出为 MP4／GIF。iOS 的 GIF 所选总时长最多 30 秒，本地 HLS 离线包不能导出。Android 与 iOS 的具体菜单有所不同。

遇到问题可先看[常见问题](faq.md#简体中文)，或[提交反馈](https://github.com/fcjnotbad-source/videoload-app/issues/new/choose)，注明平台、应用版本和操作步骤。
