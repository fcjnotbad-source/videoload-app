# FAQ / 常见问题

## English

### Is VideoLoad free? Is Pro a subscription?

Store editions are free to start. Pro is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks local video import, two-video comparison, compatible clip export and removal of the two-unfinished-task limit. See the local store for pricing. The direct Android edition has separate feature rules; see [Downloads](downloads.md).

### What counts toward the free download limit?

Unfinished tasks, including paused and failed tasks. Completed and cancelled tasks do not count. A task limit is not a guarantee of connection count or download speed.

### Does it remove ads from websites?

VideoLoad places no ads in its own interface. Third-party websites can show their own ads; the app does not promise an ad-free web.

### Are my videos uploaded?

The app does not require uploading media to the developer for playback or clip processing. Media and clip marks are kept locally. Browsing and downloading still contact the websites and media servers you choose. Those sites and the app stores have their own privacy policies. See the [Android](https://fcjnotbad-source.github.io/videoload-review-media/privacy.html) and [iOS](https://fcjnotbad-source.github.io/videoload-review-media/ios/privacy.html) policies.

### Can it download every video or website?

No. Compatibility depends on the website, source server and format. Only download content you own or are authorized to save. VideoLoad does not bypass DRM, paywalls or access controls. The Google Play edition does not support YouTube downloads.

### Why did a download stop in the background?

Network access, source expiry and system scheduling can affect transfers. On iOS, force-quitting stops transfers. Reopen the app and resume a supported source; reuse of downloaded data depends on the format and server. If the problem repeats, report your platform, version and the steps you took.

### Can I export every downloaded video?

No. Export requires Pro and a compatible source. On iOS, local HLS offline packages support in-app playback but not MP4/GIF export, file sharing or saving to Photos. iOS GIF selections are limited to 30 seconds in total. An MP4 source is a good starting point for the [demo](quick-start.md).

### Are the two players synchronized frame by frame?

They have independent controls. Two-video comparison does not guarantee frame-accurate synchronization.

### Can I use one purchase on Android and iPhone?

Purchases are platform-specific. Android and iOS store accounts and purchases are separate; no cross-platform unlock is offered. Use the purchasing store account to restore access, and use Restore Purchases on iOS.

### Is this the application's source repository?

This repository publishes product information, images and support forms. The application source is private. You can still report problems and suggest features here in English or Chinese.

### How do I get help?

[Choose a feedback form](https://github.com/fcjnotbad-source/videoload-app/issues/new/choose). Public reports are visible to everyone. Do not post private URLs, credentials, receipts or personal videos. For private matters, use the [developer contact page](https://vdb.aivest.art/apps/jiange-kuaixia#contact).

## 简体中文

### 免费吗？Pro 是订阅吗？

商店版可免费开始使用。Pro 是可选买断内购，无订阅，解锁本地视频导入、双屏对照、兼容来源片段导出，并取消两条未完成任务限制。价格以当地商店为准；Android 直装版规则不同，见[下载说明](downloads.md#简体中文)。

### 两条下载任务怎么计算？

只计算未完成任务，暂停和失败也计入；已完成或已取消不计入。任务数量不代表保证的连接数量或下载速度。

### 网页里的广告也会消失吗？

应用自身界面不投放广告，第三方网页可能有自己的广告，不承诺网页完全无广告。

### 视频会上传吗？

播放、片段处理无需把媒体上传给开发者，媒体与标注保留在本机。浏览和下载仍会连接你选择的网站和媒体服务器，相关网站及应用商店有各自的隐私政策。详见 [Android](https://fcjnotbad-source.github.io/videoload-review-media/privacy.html) 与 [iOS](https://fcjnotbad-source.github.io/videoload-review-media/ios/privacy-zh.html) 隐私政策。

### 所有网站、视频都能下载吗？

不能保证。兼容性取决于网站、来源服务器和格式。请仅下载自己拥有或已获授权保存的内容，应用不会绕过 DRM、付费墙或访问限制。Google Play 版不支持 YouTube 下载。

### 为什么切到后台后停止下载？

网络状态、来源过期和系统调度都可能影响下载。iOS 强制退出会停止传输；重新打开后可尝试继续受支持来源，能否复用已下载数据取决于格式和服务器。反复出现时，请反馈平台、版本和复现步骤。

### 所有下载视频都能导出吗？

导出需要 Pro，并取决于来源兼容性。iOS 本地 HLS 离线包可在应用内播放，但不能导出 MP4/GIF、分享文件或保存到相册；iOS GIF 所选总时长最多 30 秒。可先用[演示 MP4](quick-start.md#简体中文)体验。

### 双屏是逐帧同步的吗？

两个播放器独立控制，不保证逐帧同步。

### 买一次可以跨 Android 和 iPhone 吗？

不同平台的账号和购买权益独立，不提供跨平台解锁。恢复已有购买请使用原商店账号；iOS 可在 Pro 页选择“恢复购买”。

### 这里是应用源码库吗？

这里公开产品介绍、图片和反馈入口，应用源码仍为私有。欢迎用中文或英文报告问题、提出建议。

### 如何反馈？

[选择反馈表单](https://github.com/fcjnotbad-source/videoload-app/issues/new/choose)。公开反馈所有人可见，请勿提交私人网址、凭据、收据或私人视频；不便公开的事项请通过[开发者联系页](https://vdb.aivest.art/apps/jiange-kuaixia#contact)联系。
